# Content Pillars

Starting allocation; revise using performance and user feedback.

1. Agentic AI and LLMs — 35%
   Claude, skills, MCP, orchestration, model routing,
   agentic workflows and practical AI applications.

2. Software Engineering — 30%
   Angular, TypeScript, Nx, micro-frontends, testing,
   CI/CD and developer productivity.

3. Software Architecture — 20%
   System design, trade-offs, scalability,
   maintainability and engineering decisions.

4. Personal Engineering Journey — 15%
   Experiments, conference takeaways, lessons learned,
   mistakes and professional development.

## Audience
- Software engineers
- Senior engineers
- Engineering managers
- AI practitioners
- Technical recruiters

## Principles
- Practical over theoretical.
- Experience over generic opinions.
- Explain trade-offs.
- Prefer original insight.
- Do not invent facts or personal stories.
- Avoid repeating recent topics without a new angle.
- Use research to inform content, not to manufacture personal opinions.
- Do not comment on every technology announcement.
